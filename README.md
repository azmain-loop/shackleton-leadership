## Open it
- Double-click `index.html` (Chrome or Edge), or use VS Code's Live Server extension.
- Keep `index.html` and the `img` folder together.
- Connect to the internet: fonts and the Part 4 Prezi illustrations load online.

## Controls
→ / Space / clicker: next · ←: back · 0: black opening · 1–8: jump to a part
N: speaker notes · P: photo · M: chart · F: full screen · H: hide text

## Editing
- Story scenes: `const CH = [` · Part 4: `PZ_STEPS`, `PZ_GROUPS` · Part 5: `SL_STEPS`, `SL_HTML`, `SLQ` · Parts 6–8: `TR_STEPS`
- Colours and fonts: the `:root` block at the top of the `<style>`.
